## Deliverables & Evaluation Components

| Week | Milestone | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | Problem Identification | Define the problem statement, objectives, and deliverables. |
| Week 3–4 | Literature Survey | Identify at least 10 references from IEEE/Scopus-indexed journals and other relevant research sources. |
| Week 5–6 | Feasibility Study | Prepare the block diagram and select components, platforms, and tools. |
| Week 7–8 | System Design | Prepare flow diagrams, flowcharts, system architecture, and simulation models. |
| Week 9–10 | Design Validation | Perform requirement verification, performance testing, prototype testing, and functional simulation. |
| Week 11–12 | Report Drafting | Prepare the interim report covering the literature review, design, analysis, and simulation. |
| Week 13–14 | Presentation & Viva | Present the project for internal review by the project committee. |

## Major Project–I: Project Proposal Template

### 1. Title Page
- Project Title
- Team Members (Names & Roll Numbers)
- Guide Name and Designation
- Department and Institution Name
- Date / Academic Year

### 2. Problem Statement
- Brief description of the problem being addressed.
- Motivation and societal relevance.

### 3. Objectives
- Clear, measurable, and time-bound goals.

### 4. Literature Review
- Summary of related work (at least 10 references).
- Research gap analysis.

### 5. Proposed Methodology
- System architecture / block diagram.
- Description of the working principle.

### 6. Tools and Technologies
- Software, hardware, simulation environments, and libraries.

### 7. Expected Outcomes
- Simulations, models, diagrams, and validation metrics.

### 8. Timeline
- Weekly plan with milestones (Gantt chart preferred).

### 9. References
- IEEE referencing format preferred.

| System Design and Analysis | 10 | High | Architecture, feasibility, and correctness. |
| Simulation / Theoretical Validation | 10 | Medium | Valid tools and representative results. |
